Questions

Does ZeroLatency send email without asking me?

Only for the kinds of replies you approve. Everything else waits as a draft, and every send can be undone for 10 seconds.

Which email providers work?

Gmail and Google Workspace today. Outlook is planned.

Do I have to move my email?

No. ZeroLatency works on top of your existing account; nothing is migrated.

Is my mail used to train models?

No. Your mail is processed only to act for you.
