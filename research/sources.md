## Access notes (please read)
1. **Notion Mail shut down on 2026-09-22**, three days before this research ([S6], [S14]). `notion.com/product/mail` now redirects to the Notion homepage ([S9]).
2. **The help articles are gone too.** In a normal browser on 2026-09-25, all nine Mail help URLs checked ([S1]–[S6], plus "Get started", "Notion Mail for mobile" and the auto-labeling guide) redirect to `notion.com/help`. The text cited from [S1]–[S6] comes from WebFetch's copies of those pages, which are no longer reachable live. Treat those citations as "last available version".
3. **Screenshots.** The cloud sandbox's egress proxy blocks notion.com, images.ctfassets.net and the review sites (HTTP 403), so headless Playwright could not be used. The Lead approved the user's in-app browser (Claude Browser pane) instead. In that browser, official image assets were fetched from the live pages [S7], [S8] and [S9] with JavaScript, re-encoded (GIFs as one frame), transferred as base64 and saved as PNG. Notion's CSP blocked html2canvas, so there are no page-level captures. I did not use web.archive.org or any other mirror.
4. The Verge's launch article (David Pierce, 2025-04-15; listed on Techmeme) was **not read**, because the search tool returned an error. I cite it nowhere.
